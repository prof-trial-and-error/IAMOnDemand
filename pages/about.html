About IAM On Demand

Simplifying identity and access management for businesses of all sizes.

Our Mission

At IAM On Demand, we believe that secure, enterprise-grade identity management shouldn't require a dedicated team of specialists or millions in infrastructure investment. Our mission is to democratize access to world-class IAM solutions by providing fully managed Keycloak hosting that just works.

Why Keycloak?

Keycloak is an open-source identity and access management solution backed by Red Hat. It's trusted by thousands of organizations worldwide for its robust feature set, active community, and enterprise-ready capabilities. By building on Keycloak, we ensure you're never locked into proprietary solutions.

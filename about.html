About Us

Making Identity and Access Management as easy as pie!

Welcome to IAM-OnDemand, where we make Identity and Access Management (IAM) as easy as pie! Founded by two IT security professionals in Germany, we realized that commercial IAM solutions are often too expensive and come with a steep learning curve. That's why we decided to combine the best of both worlds: the power of Keycloak, an open-source IAM solution, with the ease and comfort of commercial SaaS solutions.

Our Story

Once upon a time, in a land not so far away (Germany, to be exact), two IT security experts were frustrated. They saw how expensive commercial IAM solutions were and how complex Keycloak could be for new users. They thought, "There must be a better way!" And so, IAM-OnDemand was born.

The Problem

Managing identity and access can be a real headache. Commercial solutions can cost an arm and a leg, and setting up Keycloak on your own? Well, let's just say it's not a walk in the park. Many organizations struggle with the cost, complexity, and maintenance of their IAM systems.

Our Solution

At IAM-OnDemand, we've taken the robust, open-source Keycloak and made it as easy to use as your favorite app. No more steep learning curves, no more sky-high costs. Just secure, scalable, and hassle-free IAM hosting.

Why Choose Us?

  • Easy Peasy Setup: Pick your tier, place your order, and fire up your Keycloak IAM system in minutes.
  • Compliance Made Simple: We help you master GDPR, SOC2, ISO 27001, and more with central logging, auditing, and strong authentication.
  • Scalability & Automation: Centralize user identity so all your apps rely on one user directory and one login flow.
  • Expertise You Can Trust: Our team has deployed Keycloak for SaaS, enterprise, and regulated industries.
  • Proven Plan: A straightforward 3-step onboarding to get you live in days, not months.

Our Promise

We understand your struggle. Scaling Keycloak for growing user bases without downtime or skyrocketing costs. Ensuring continuous security patching and version upgrades without breaking existing integrations. Meeting enterprise compliance demands while moving fast.

With IAM-OnDemand, you get a fully managed Keycloak hosting solution that takes care of everything from configuration to ongoing monitoring. You can focus on building the products your customers care about, while we ensure your identity infrastructure just works.
